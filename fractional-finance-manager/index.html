Services

A fractional finance manager, when you need senior support.

Senior finance support without necessarily needing a full-time finance hire.

What this covers

A fractional arrangement gives you experienced finance input for a defined amount of time. It can cover:

  • Oversight of bookkeeping and reporting
  • Regular review of financial information
  • Support with budgets and cash flow
  • A consistent point of contact for finance questions

Who it is for

Growing businesses that need finance leadership but not a full-time appointment.

How we start

We begin with a conversation about your circumstances, what is already in place and what you would like to be clearer about. There is no need to have everything organised beforehand.
