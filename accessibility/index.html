Accessibility

Last updated: 13 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. This statement explains what we have done and how to tell us about a problem.

Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The site has not yet been independently audited, so we cannot claim full conformance.

What we have built in

  • A “skip to content” link at the top of every page.
  • Menus, dropdowns and the cookie controls that can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Clear headings and page landmarks so screen reader users can move around easily.
  • Colour choices made with text contrast in mind.
  • A layout that adapts to different screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • Reduced animation if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Descriptive link text, and text alternatives for meaningful images.

Known limitations

We are not aware of any barriers at the time of writing. Because the site has not been formally audited, there may be problems we have not found. If you meet one, please tell us.

Tell us about a problem

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, or need information in another format such as large print, plain text or audio, email info@chariotfinancialservices.com or call 07497 528077. Please tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within five working days.

If you are not happy with our response

You can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service, which advises on discrimination and equality issues.

About this statement
