Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies used on this website, and how to control them.

What they are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in a comparable way. We use as few as we can.

What we use

Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them. Nothing from Google is requested until you do.

Analytics

If you accept, we load Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read and how people arrive. We use this to improve the website. We do not use analytics to identify you. You can read how Google handles this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether to allow analytics. You can accept, reject or manage your preferences. You can change your mind at any time:

If you withdraw consent, we stop collecting analytics data and remove the Google Analytics cookies we can reach. The change takes full effect when you load the next page. You can also delete cookies or block them in your browser settings, though some features may then behave differently.
