Professional Standards

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This page sets out the professional standards that apply to our work.

Our professional body

Chariot Financial Services Limited is led by Sanjoy Kumar Roy, a member of ACCA, the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants. As an ACCA member he is bound by ACCA’s rules, including its Code of Ethics and Conduct.

Ethics and confidentiality

The Code of Ethics and Conduct is built on five fundamental principles: integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality, and professional behaviour. We treat everything clients tell us as confidential, and we disclose it only with permission or where the law or our professional obligations require it.
