Management accounts, designed to be used.

Regular financial information designed to help you understand performance and make decisions.

What this covers

Management accounts turn bookkeeping into something you can act on. They can include:

  • Regular profit and loss and balance sheet reporting
  • Cash flow visibility
  • Commentary on what the numbers are showing
  • Information tailored to the decisions you face

Who it is for

Owner-managed businesses that want better information than an annual set of accounts provides.

How we start

We begin with a conversation about your circumstances, what is already in place and what you would like to be clearer about. There is no need to have everything organised beforehand.
