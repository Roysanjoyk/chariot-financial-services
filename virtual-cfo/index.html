Services

A virtual CFO, for a higher-level view.

Higher-level financial perspective for businesses that need more strategic support.

What this covers

A virtual CFO arrangement focuses on direction rather than only compliance. It can include:

  • Financial strategy discussions
  • Reporting that supports management and board decisions
  • Review of financial controls and processes
  • Planning for growth and change

Who it is for

Businesses that need strategic financial perspective without a permanent CFO.

How we start

Start with a conversation about where you are and what you would like clearer. There is no need to have everything organised beforehand.
