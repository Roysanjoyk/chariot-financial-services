What happens when your income goes above £100,000?

The Personal Allowance taper can create an unexpectedly high marginal tax rate. Here is what the threshold means.

The taper in brief

At the time of writing, the Personal Allowance is reduced by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income above £100,000. With the standard allowance, it is fully withdrawn at £125,140.

Why it matters

Within that band, each extra pound of income can lead to a higher effective rate of tax than the headline rates suggest, because you are also losing allowance. Many people are surprised by how steep it is.

Rates, thresholds and rules differ between parts of the UK and change over time. Check current GOV.UK guidance, or talk to us about your own position.
