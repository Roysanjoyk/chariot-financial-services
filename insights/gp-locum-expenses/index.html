GP locum expenses: what can you actually claim?

A practical guide to the types of expenditure that may be relevant to a GP locum, and why keeping good records matters.

The general principle

Broadly, expenses need to be incurred for the purposes of your business. Whether a particular cost qualifies depends on the facts, so treat the list below as areas to consider, not a guarantee.

Areas that may be relevant

  • Professional indemnity and related subscriptions
  • Professional registration fees and memberships
  • Training and professional development
  • Medical equipment and supplies
  • Travel, noting that ordinary commuting is treated differently from business travel
  • Insurance relevant to the business
  • Accountancy fees
  • A reasonable share of home office costs where appropriate

Why records matter

Keep receipts, invoices and a clear note of the business purpose. Good records make your return easier to prepare and easier to support if questions arise.

Talk it through

If you are unsure about a particular cost, ask before you claim it.

This page gives general information only and is not personal advice. Rules, rates and thresholds change, so check current guidance before acting.
